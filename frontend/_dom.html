Image-assisted anemia screening

HerHealth AIΓÇÖs first screening feature works with two kinds of photos: a close-up of your inner lower eyelid (conjunctiva), and a close-up of a fingernail or nail bed. Changes in pallor can be associated with anemia ΓÇö but photos alone cannot measure hemoglobin or confirm anything.

Inner lower eyelid

A well-lit, close-up photo of the inside of your lower eyelid (conjunctiva). Pale conjunctiva can be associated with anemia.

Look straight ahead, gently pull the lower lid down, use natural light, no flash or filters.

Γ£ô

Fingernail & nail bed

A close-up photo of a clean, unpolished fingernail and the nail bed beneath it.

Natural nail polish only, daylight, hold the camera steady, avoid zoom.

Honest about what this is ΓÇö and isnΓÇÖt

Real AI analysis is not available yet. No model has been validated or integrated, so this will never return a fabricated anemia probability. A blood test is the only way to confirm anemia. This feature exists to help you notice patterns and prepare for a conversation with a clinician.

Example 1 ┬╖ Inner lower eyelid

Well-lit and unfiltered. Skin tone, lighting, and camera settings all affect how pale an eyelid looks.

Example 2 ┬╖ Fingernail and nail bed

Natural nails only, daylight, no filters. Nail polish and colour on the skin change what a camera captures.
